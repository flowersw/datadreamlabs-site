# PRODUCT — datadreamlabs.com

register: brand

## Purpose
Will Flowers's professional home on the domain his email uses (will@datadreamlabs.com). Its first job is to reassure someone who clicked through that this is a credible, reachable senior engineer. Its second is to leave a door open for contract work under the DataDream Labs name.

## Users
1. Recruiters and hiring managers (primary). On a laptop between calls, spending about 20 seconds deciding "credible? what kind of role? how do I reach him?"
2. Prospective contract clients (secondary). Want to know what problems he takes on and how to start.

## Tone
Steady, specific, hands-on. Plain claims backed by one proof each. No hype, no "passionate," no AI-generated gloss.

## Constraints
- Claims must match the canonical résumés (`output/resume-source/build_resumes.py` in the job-search workspace). No Pangia business metrics on the public site.
- No phone number or street address in public pages.
- Static HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages; no build step.

## Anti-references
Generic dev-portfolio templates (dark navy plus neon, terminal cosplay, project-card grids), SaaS landing hero metrics, editorial serif magazine look.
